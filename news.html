Published 9 August 2026
Diagnostics · 2026, 16(16), 2514

Comparison of LC-MS/MS and CLIA methods for vitamin D measurement in an unselected outpatient cohort: statistical evaluation and impact on clinical classification

Co-first author · Corbetta, Corsaro, Caradonna, Rusconi, … de Grazia

Two high-throughput platforms measuring 25-hydroxyvitamin D on the same 1064 paired outpatient samples disagree enough to matter: the immunoassay flags deficiency in 546 samples against 312 for mass spectrometry, so the choice of platform, not the patient, decides a large share of the supplementation decisions.

Misura di Vitamina D con due piattaforme analitiche ad alta automazione e differente tecnologia in una coorte di pazienti ambulatoriali: valutazione statistica e impatto sulla classificazione clinica

Automated classification of BRCA1/2 variants by a multi-agent ACMG/AMP system: validation vs. molecular geneticist on 170 cases

Poster · First author, presenting in person

A multi-agent system that applies the ACMG/AMP criteria to germline BRCA1/2 variants, validated against a molecular geneticist on 170 cases. Substantial agreement and full sensitivity on pathogenic variants, positioned as decision support rather than replacement.
